# /kickoff — Project Kickoff

Interactive kickoff interview covering:
1. What problem is being solved?
2. Who are the users?
3. What does success look like?
4. What are the constraints? (tech stack, timeline, team)
5. What are the risks?

After interview, generate PRD at `docs/prd/[project-name].md` using `docs/templates/prd_template.md`.
