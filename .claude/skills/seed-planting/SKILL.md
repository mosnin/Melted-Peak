# Seed Planting

Capture future ideas in `memory/seeds/[date]-[name].md` with trigger conditions.

**Seed format:** Title, Why, When to Surface (specific triggers), Breadcrumbs (related files/decisions/state), Notes.

**Statuses:** planted → sprouted → harvested | wilted

**Checking:** Seeds are scanned during /boot and /plan. If trigger conditions match current work, the seed is presented to the user.

**Never act on a seed immediately.** Plant it, continue current work.

See `skills/seed-planting/source/SKILL.md` for full format and workflow.
